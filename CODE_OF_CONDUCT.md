# Community conduct

Shipit SEO welcomes contributors of different backgrounds, experience levels
and languages. Help others participate and keep feedback focused on the work.

- Be respectful. Explain disagreements with concrete examples.
- Accept good-faith questions and corrections. Do not ridicule someone for
  their knowledge, language or mistakes.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or target another person.
- Do not publish private information, credentials or screenshots that expose
  someone else's data.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam or repeated unwanted contact.

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and
discussions, including when representing the project elsewhere.

## Report a concern

Email **info@shipitseo.com** with links or a short account of what happened.
Include only information needed to review the concern. Avoid posting private
details in a public thread.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close a conversation or
restrict participation. Responses should reflect the conduct and its impact.
Reports will be handled discreetly, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed
when GitHub moderation or legal obligations require disclosure.
